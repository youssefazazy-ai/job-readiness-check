# E-commerce & Product Research Specialist (Freelance)

**Location:** Remote  
**Employment Type:** Freelance / Project-Based  
**Market Focus:** Egypt and the United States

## About the Project
I'm building two e-commerce businesses: a clothing brand targeting the Egyptian market and a dropshipping store targeting customers in the United States, with products sourced from China.

I'm looking for a proactive, research-oriented specialist who can identify promising products, analyze competitors, validate suppliers, and help turn research into practical launch plans.

## Responsibilities
- Research trending and evergreen products using Amazon, TikTok, Meta Ads Library, Google Trends, and other relevant tools.
- Analyze competitors, pricing, customer reviews, and market demand.
- Identify and evaluate suppliers and manufacturers in China and Egypt.
- Compare product costs, shipping, customs duties, and fulfillment options.
- Estimate profit margins, advertising costs, and break-even acquisition costs.
- Help validate product ideas before significant capital is committed.
- Prepare clear research reports with actionable recommendations.

## Requirements
- Experience in e-commerce, dropshipping, or product research.
- Familiarity with Shopify and product research tools.
- Strong analytical and spreadsheet skills.
- Understanding of supplier verification and international shipping.
- Ability to support decisions with real data rather than assumptions.
- Clear communication and reliable execution.

## Deliverables
1. A shortlist of 10 potential products for the US market.
2. A competitor analysis for each shortlisted product.
3. Supplier options with quotations and shipping details.
4. A landed-cost and profit-margin calculator.
5. A recommended testing plan with a realistic budget.
6. A separate product research report for the Egyptian clothing brand.

## Application
Please include:
- Relevant experience and examples of previous work.
- Tools you use for product research.
- A sample research report, if available.
- Your proposed fee and estimated delivery time.

**Important:** The initial project is research and validation. No large inventory purchases or advertising expenditure will be authorized without approval.
