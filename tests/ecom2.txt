# Job Title: Winning Product Researcher for US Dropshipping Store

**Job Type:** Freelance / Project-Based  
**Target Market:** United States  
**Sourcing Location:** China

## Job Description

I’m looking for an experienced product researcher to help me identify and validate promising products for a dropshipping business targeting customers in the United States, with suppliers primarily based in China.

The goal is to find products with real market demand, competitive advantages, reliable suppliers, and realistic profit margins—not just viral products or random trending items.

## Responsibilities

- Research potential products using TikTok, Meta Ads Library, Amazon, Google Trends, and competitor stores.
- Analyze demand, competition, pricing, customer reviews, and advertising angles.
- Identify reliable Chinese suppliers and compare product costs, shipping times, and fulfillment options.
- Calculate estimated landed costs, selling prices, gross margins, and break-even advertising costs.
- Evaluate potential risks, including returns, product quality, shipping delays, and US import duties.
- Recommend the best products to test with a limited budget.

## Required Deliverables

1. A shortlist of 10 potential products.
2. Competitor and market analysis for each product.
3. Supplier links and available quotations.
4. Estimated profit calculations for each product.
5. Recommended selling price and target customer.
6. A prioritized shortlist of the best 2–3 products to test first.
7. A practical testing plan with a proposed budget.

## Requirements

- Proven experience in US dropshipping or e-commerce product research.
- Ability to support recommendations with real data and evidence.
- Familiarity with Chinese suppliers and international fulfillment.
- Strong analytical and communication skills.
- Experience with Shopify is a plus.

## How to Apply

Please include:

- Your relevant experience and previous results.
- Examples of product research you have completed.
- The tools and methods you use to validate products.
- Your fixed price for this project and estimated completion time.

**Important:** This is initially a research and validation project. No product purchases, advertising campaigns, or major spending will be authorized without my approval.
